Overview
--------

Here is a collection of my notes. Most of my knowledge, such as how certain
systems work together, is expressed as code and comments throughout my projects.
A more general and architectural knowledge, which requires a greater
explanation, is collected here in this website.

Here is how I organize my knowledge:

- All my code is hosted on `github <https://github.com/San7o/>`_ and
  `gist.github <https://gist.github.com/San7o>`_

- I have a `me <https://san7o.github.io/giovanni-diary/me.html>`_ page with an
  overview of my major projects

- Technical notes are here in this website

- `Giovanni's Diary <https://san7o.github.io/giovanni-diary/v2/>`_ contains
  non-technical blog posts and projects

- Private notes are saved `here <https://github.com/San7o/private>`_ (only I can
  read it)

- I use `mastodon <https://mastodon.social/@santo7>`_ as social media. I have
  other accounts like Linkedin, Reddit, etc, but I don't post much (yet).

You can email me at `giovanni.santini@proton.me <mailto:giovanni.santini@proton.me>`_
