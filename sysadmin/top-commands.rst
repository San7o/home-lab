Top commands
============

See what is taking up resources in your system:

- `s-tui`: stressing out cpu
- `htop`: like top
- `atop`: technical and lots of metrics
- `iftop`: network traffic
- `iotop`: disk top
- `nvtop`: GPU top
- `btop`: colors
- `wavemon`: watch wifi signal strength
- `slabtop`: show slab objects

Other very useful and related commands:

- `lstopo`: show the topology of the motherboard
