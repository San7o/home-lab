Camerino Group¶

Here are some notes about the new group that we are aiming to create.

I have recognized some initial issues that we should tackle. Once we have 4 or 5 people interested, we should have a meeting at the university where we discuss these issues together, listen to everyone’s interests and ideas about the group. This would be the “founding committee” which defines the group before officially advertising it and organizing events.

I think there is great value in making like-minded people come together. Especially in an university, where we are here to educate. But the group is open to any curious that wishes to learn, whether they are affiliated with the university or not. I hope that this group will act as a incubator and contamination place to new and experienced hackers.

Goals¶

  • diffondere una culura di curiosita’ nel mondo informatico

  • diffondere una cultura del software libero

  • percorso di crescita, educazione, aggregazione e contaminazione tra i propri membri ed esterni

  • incubatore di idee e tecnologie

  • riconoscere l’universita’ di camerino come polo di eccellenza tecnologico e di innovazione nelle marche

Issue 1: Who we are¶

The main issue now is to define who we are. This can be divided into the sub issues listed below.

Sub-Issue 1¶

Do we identify ourself as a LUG (Linux User Group) or do we shape our own destiny?

  • If we organize as a LUG, then we have a lot of structure already established for us: we have examples for the statute and guidelines, it is easier to present ourself since passionate people already know about LUGs and are already socialized in them; we also have some people already interested from the old Camerino LUG. Additionally, we would also have periodic global events to participate in (like the “Linux Day”) while taking part on a bigger community of Linux and free software enthusiasts, both inside and outside the university. I think this is valuable and it puts us in a bigger and international context. I am not too found of the free software ideology honestly, which is the only negative thing about being a LUG. However, in the spirit of LUGs, we should talk and promote free software as a core theme of the group.

  • if we don’t organize as a LUG (such as, an “hackerspace”) then we need to define our own identity and guidelines, with much more freedom. I think this is more difficult unless we have really good ideas.

Sub-Issue 2¶

How do we formalize the group? The ideas are:

  • as am official LUG. We (me) should research how to do this.

  • as a subgroup of the TODO association. I am concerned about the independence of the group, I don’t want its organization and administration to depend of another entity that may limit our freedom and self reliance.

Issue 2: What do we want to do¶

We should discuss ideas about what to do. For example:

  • periodic events where anyone can present a talk about any topic, such as a project they have been working on, a war story or an interesting thing they learned. I could talk about the Linux development process and do several (infinitely many) talk about the various subsystems in the kernel.

  • workshops / hackaton, where we all program something and explore ideas

  • invited guests: working people, researchers and professors can present a talk about a topic they choose.
